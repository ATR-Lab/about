## How this list was made

- **Source.** Authors and titles come from the [lab website's publications page](https://www.atr.cs.kent.edu/research/publications/), retrieved Sept. 30, 2026. The website lists the director's earlier work too; this selection starts in 2017, the year the lab opened. The VendoBot paper (2026) is not on the website yet.
- **Checks.** Each selected entry was matched to its DOI record through [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/) on Sept. 30, 2026. Only entries whose authors and title match the publisher's record are included. Venue names, pages and DOIs follow the DOI record; conference proceedings published the following year show that publication year.
- **Style.** Formatted per the [IEEE Reference Guide](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/IEEE-Reference-Guide.pdf): initials before surnames, up to six authors, "et al." after the first author when there are more than six. Titles keep the capitalization the website uses.
- **Names.** Author names come from the published record. Listing an author here does not make them a current lab member; the member list is in [members/README.md](members/README.md).
