# Director bio

> **Read this when** a program, press release, event listing or introduction needs a short bio of the lab's director.

This is the 50-word bio template for Jong-Hoon Kim, director of the Advanced Telerobotics Research Lab. The bracketed parts can be filled **only** from the director's approved CV: never from older web pages, search results or memory. Until the brackets are filled and the director approves the text, it is a draft.

## Template

Status: **draft template**. Brackets stay until the director supplies the approved CV wording.

> Jong-Hoon Kim is an associate professor of computer science at Kent State University and directs the Advanced Telerobotics Research Lab. Kim's research focuses on [areas from the approved CV]. [One verified recognition.] Kim holds a Ph.D. in computer science from Louisiana State University.

## How to fill it

- [ ] Get the current approved CV from the director. Re-check the title every semester (associate professor since August 2023).
- [ ] Replace [areas from the approved CV] with the research areas exactly as the CV words them.
- [ ] Replace [One verified recognition.] with one recognition that the CV states and that has a source. Use the exact award name the conferring body uses (see [achievements.md](../achievements.md) for the never-upgrade rules).
- [ ] Keep it near 50 words, in the third person, in AP style: full name on first reference, "Kim" after that, no "Dr.", title lowercase after the name.
- [ ] Don't add career totals (publication counts, grant totals, patents): they differ by source.
- [ ] Get the director's written approval, then mark the status here as approved, with the date.
