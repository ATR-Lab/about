# Photos

> **Read this when** you add a photo or video to the press kit, or a reporter asks for images of the Advanced Telerobotics Research Laboratory.

The press kit needs 6 to 10 approved photos and 1 to 3 minutes of b-roll (clean background footage). None is here yet: **[to be supplied]**. A photo goes in only when it has a caption, alt text, a credit and a release on file. When in doubt about consent, leave the photo out.

## What we need

- [ ] 6 to 10 photos of the lab's own work (real photos, not illustrations)
- [ ] Landscape and portrait versions of the strongest images
- [ ] Robots in action (doing the task, not parked), with the robot at eye level and a calm background
- [ ] Teleoperation shots that show the link: the operator at the controls and the robot responding in one frame, or as a matched pair
- [ ] Environmental portraits of members at their bench or with their robot, with the member's written permission
- [ ] A high-resolution headshot of the director [to be supplied] (see [director-bio.md](../director-bio.md))
- [ ] Safe practice in every frame: safety glasses for soldering, cutting, drilling and battery work; closed-toe shoes

Kent State's photo style: "use available natural light when possible, demonstrate purpose and engagement and avoid staging photos" ([kent.edu/brand/images](https://www.kent.edu/brand/images)).

File names: lowercase kebab-case that describes the scene, never a person's name, for example `pepper-demo-open-lab-landscape.jpg`.

## Consent and releases

> [!WARNING]
> **Minors:** no photo of anyone under 18 without a signed parent or guardian release on file. Never name a minor in a caption, alt text or file name, and never pair a face with a school name. If you can't tell whether someone is a minor, treat them as one.

- **Kent State students:** name a student in a caption only with the student's written permission (Kent State's web guidance: [kent.edu/ucm/web-team/images-graphics-and-video](https://www.kent.edu/ucm/web-team/images-graphics-and-video)).
- **Visitors, partners, teachers and family members:** anyone who is not a Kent State student or employee signs a model release before their photo is used ([kent.edu/ucm/photography-and-videography](https://www.kent.edu/ucm/photography-and-videography)).
- **Human-subjects studies:** use study photos or footage only if the IRB-approved consent form covers publicity use; ask the director.
- **Location data:** strip GPS metadata before a file goes in this folder.
- **Take-down requests:** if someone asks for a photo to come down, remove it promptly and note it in the photo log.
- **Kent State photographers:** UCM photo and video shoots need 3 to 4 weeks' notice. UCM's archive photos are university property; use them only with permission.

## Captions, alt text and credits

| Field | Rule | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Caption | What, who (with consent) and where, in the present tense, plus any integrity label: "(teleoperated)", "(autonomous)", "(simulation)" or "(2× speed)" | `[Robot or system] [does what] during [event or experiment], [Month Day], at Kent State University.` |
| Alt text | One or two sentences: who or what, doing what, where, and the detail that makes the point. Don't start with "image of" | Describe the actual photo; don't reuse a sample sentence |
| Credit | Lab-shot photos: "Photo: ATR Lab, Kent State University" or a named photographer. UCM photos: as UCM specifies. Third-party images need permission and a credit | `Photo: [photographer name]` or `Image: [owner], used with permission` |

Never present an AI-generated image as a real lab photo. Illustrations are captioned "Illustration" and stay out of this folder.

## B-roll

- [ ] 1 to 3 minutes of clean lab footage, 1080p or 4K
- [ ] Every clip labeled for playback speed (for example "2× speed") and for control mode: teleoperated, autonomous, semi-autonomous or scripted
- [ ] No music, no unlicensed footage, no whiteboards or screens showing unpublished results or sponsor material
- [ ] Captions on any clip with speech

Store b-roll where the lab keeps large media [b-roll storage location] and link it here; don't commit video files to this repository.
