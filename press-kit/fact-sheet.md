## Research focus

- **In the lab's own words:** "An innovation research laboratory focused on exploring the frontiers of telepresence robotics, tele-embodiment, autonomy and artificial intelligence."
- **Direction:** from telepresence, tele-embodiment and immersive teleoperation toward Physical AI (AI that senses and acts in the real world through a robot).
- **Plain terms:** telepresence means driving a robot from somewhere else, seeing and hearing through it; tele-embodiment means feeling as if you are inside the robot's body while you control it; teleoperation means controlling a robot from a distance.

## Current projects and platforms

| Project | One line |
|---|---|
| VendoBot | A projection-augmented delivery robot for improving intent transparency (showing people what the robot plans to do next) and motion legibility in service robots; published in the HRI 2026 Companion proceedings (Edinburgh, March 2026) |
| Coffee Buddy | An interactive service robot for coffee shop settings |
| PepperKit | A SoftBank Pepper robot with a realtime LLM voice agent (an AI language model that listens and answers aloud; its answers can be wrong) |
| Expressive Motion | Expressive walking for a Booster K1 humanoid, learned from video of a person walking (2026) |

**Platforms:** SoftBank Pepper, Unitree Go2, Booster K1 humanoid, a Yahboom ROS 2 robot and a robot mower, plus the recent TeleBot generations TeleBot-3R and TeleBot-4R (with an XR interface for Meta Quest).

**Past work (with years):** ImmersiFLY, an immersive pilot training simulator (2017 to about 2019); TeleBot-2 (World Robot Summit, 2018); the Telesuit control suit (2019); the AARON augmented reality system for NASA SUITS (2020, 2023).
