# Boilerplates

> **Read this when** you need a ready-made paragraph about the Advanced Telerobotics Research Laboratory: the "About" section of a press release, a speaker bio, an event listing, a partner page or a social profile.

These are the lab's canonical boilerplates, copied verbatim from its brand fact base. Use them unchanged. Only the formal one-liner is the lab's own published wording; every other version is a **draft** until the director approves it, and after approval every channel uses the approved text word for word.

Status: **published**. The lab's self-description as it appears on the lab's homepage, verbatim after "is". Use it whenever a draft below has not been approved.

## 100 words

Status: **draft** until the director approves.

> The Advanced Telerobotics Research Laboratory at Kent State University builds robots that extend human presence and Physical AI systems that work alongside people. Founded in 2017 in the Department of Computer Science, the lab explores telepresence robotics, tele-embodiment, autonomy and artificial intelligence. Students work on real hardware, from humanoid and quadruped robots to immersive teleoperation interfaces. Lab teams were selected as a World Robot Summit finalist (2021) and a top-10 team for NASA's onsite SUITS spacesuit-interface challenge (2020). The lab also runs summer programs for middle and high school students. Its director is Jong-Hoon Kim, associate professor of computer science.

## Mission statement

Status: **published**. First paragraph, verbatim from the [lab's mission page](https://www.atr.cs.kent.edu/atr-home/mission/). Quote it exactly; it is the one place the "(ATR)" form appears.

> The mission of the Advanced Telerobotics Research (ATR) Laboratory at Kent State University is to develop and nourish next-generation technologies that will bring people from around the world closer together. These are technologies that relate closely to “Telepresence Robotics”, and “Immersive Experiences” such as those afforded by virtual reality.

Source: [atr.cs.kent.edu/atr-home/mission](https://www.atr.cs.kent.edu/atr-home/mission/), retrieved Sept. 30, 2026.

> [!CAUTION]
> The mission page's second paragraph includes diversity wording. Check with UCM before reusing it in recruiting, hiring or scholarship copy: Ohio Senate Bill 1 limits DEI criteria in job descriptions and scholarships at Kent State.

## Variations and rules

- **Define Physical AI first.** Boilerplates sit at the end of a piece, so they don't define terms. In general-audience pieces (press releases, parent pages, sponsor one-pagers), define it in the body copy before the boilerplate: "Physical AI (AI that senses and acts in the real world through a robot)".
- **Naming the college.** Write "in the Department of Computer Science in the College of Sciences and Humanities" (seven more words). Never use the college's former name.
- **Ending with a link.** Add `Learn more at www.atr.cs.kent.edu.` On the web, use a descriptive link ("Visit the Advanced Telerobotics Research Laboratory website"), not "click here".
- **Heading.** A heading above a boilerplate may use the short form: "About the ATR Lab".
- **Kent State boilerplate.** Never write one. Request UCM's current approved paragraph. If a piece cites Kent State's R1 status, use UCM's Carnegie sentence verbatim and re-check it before release.
- **Laboratory, not Lab.** The official long form is "Advanced Telerobotics Research Laboratory" (confirmed Nov. 2026). Use it on first reference, then "the lab".
- **Re-check the director's title** every semester before reusing the 100-word version.
