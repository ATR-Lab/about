## How to word results

Use the word the source uses. This table is from the lab's design guide (never-upgrade rules).

| The source says | Write | Never write |
|---|---|---|
| Nominee, shortlisted | "nominated for", "shortlisted for" | "won", "award-winning" |
| Finalist, one of *N* finalists | "finalist", "one of *N* finalists" | "winner", "placed", "award" |
| Honorable mention | "honorable mention" | "award" on its own |
| Selected to compete | "selected to compete in" | "competed and won", "placed" |
| Selected for an onsite event | "selected for the onsite challenge" | "competed at *site*" unless the lab confirms the team was there |
| Team award | "the *team name* team received" | one student "received" it alone |
| Award to one member | "*Student*, a member of the lab, received" (with the student's written permission) | "the lab won" |
| Co-PI on a multi-institution grant | "*PI* is a co-principal investigator on *project*, funded by *agency*" | "the lab's $*total* grant", "led" |
| A workshop or special-session award | its exact title, such as "Special Session Best Award" | "Best Paper at *main conference*" |
| The director's work before Kent State (grants, media coverage) | leave it out, or date it and attribute it to the director | "lab funding", media-outlet counts credited to the lab |

## What never to claim

If you update this page or write about the lab anywhere, keep these out:

- [ ] **Any RoboCup placement or result.** None is published. RoboCup appears only as teams formed (2023) or entries planned (2019, 2022).
- [ ] **Media-outlet counts for TeleBot.** That coverage was of TeleBot-1 in 2012 to 2014, before the lab existed, and the counts vary from page to page. If you mention it at all, attribute it to the director's TeleBot research, with dates.
- [ ] **The director's career totals as lab statistics.** Publication counts, grant totals and patents belong to Jong-Hoon Kim's career, not to the lab, and the publication counts differ by source. Re-check and attribute them, or leave them out.
- [ ] **Funding totals or pre-2017 grants as lab funding.** Use `[Grant No.]` in acknowledgments until the director confirms which award funded which work.
- [ ] **Donors' names or gift amounts** without their written consent. Corporate sponsors listed publicly on the lab site may be named as past support, with the year, after the director confirms their current status.
- [ ] **Headcounts.** The website's Team page is stale. Write `[N] student researchers` until the lab supplies a dated count.
- [ ] **Upgraded results.** A finalist is not a winner, a selection is not a placement and a special-session award is not a conference best paper.

## How to add an achievement

Fill every line of this checklist before you add a row to the timeline. A lab post alone is not enough for a new claim.

- [ ] The exact name of the award or result, spelled the way the conferring body writes it
- [ ] The conferring body, the year and the date it announced
- [ ] The recipients exactly (team, named student, paper authors), each named only with consent
- [ ] What was recognized (paper title, project, entry)
- [ ] The level: winner, award, finalist, honorable mention, nominee, selected to compete, accepted
- [ ] A source from the conferring body (announcement, program, DOI or official record)
- [ ] Money only if the body published it and the director approves printing it
- [ ] For grants: the lab's role (PI, co-PI, key personnel, subaward) and the award number from the official record

Then:

1. Announce only after the conferring body has announced publicly, or has given written permission. Announce a grant only once it is official and the director approves.
2. Tell Kent State's University Communications and Marketing (UCM) before posting anything major.
3. Add the row here, newest first, with the allowed wording and the source link.
4. If the result belongs in the press kit, update [press-kit/fact-sheet.md](press-kit/fact-sheet.md) in the same pull request.
